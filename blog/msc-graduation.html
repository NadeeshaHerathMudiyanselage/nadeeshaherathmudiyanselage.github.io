Graduated with a M.Sc in Computing Sciences from Tampere University, Finland

25 April 2025

I am happy to share an important milestone in my academic journey: graduating with a Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Computing Sciences, specializing in Statistical Data Analytics, with Distinction from Tampere University, Finland.

My Academic Journey

During my master's studies, I developed a strong foundation in statistical modelling, Bayesian analysis, machine learning, artificial intelligence, data mining, and computational methods. This experience strengthened my analytical, programming, and problem-solving skills while deepening my interest in research.

My Master's Thesis

My master's thesis, "Prior Distribution Approaches on Bayesian Survival Models," focused on exploring different prior distribution approaches in Bayesian survival analysis. Working on this research helped me gain valuable experience in statistical modelling, scientific literature review, data analysis, and interpreting research findings.

A Meaningful Milestone

Graduating with Distinction is a rewarding achievement that reflects the dedication and effort I put into my studies. I am grateful to my supervisors, teachers, friends, and family for their support throughout this journey.

This experience has strengthened my passion for research and motivated me to continue exploring new opportunities in data science and computational research. I look forward to applying the knowledge and skills I have gained in the next chapter of my academic journey.
