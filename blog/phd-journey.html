From Master's Thesis to Doctoral Research

20 August 2026 · Research

My master's studies strengthened my interest in statistical modelling, Bayesian methods, and data analysis. That background now supports my doctoral research in computer science at the University of Helsinki.

My current direction

My PhD work focuses on explainable data mining algorithms and principled approaches to data analysis. I am particularly interested in methods that are not only effective, but also interpretable and theoretically well motivated.

Looking forward

I hope to use this blog to document ideas, lessons from research, and useful resources for students and researchers working in data science and computer science.
