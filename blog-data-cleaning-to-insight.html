From Data Cleaning to Insight

A beginner-friendly note on why data preparation is one of the most important parts of data analysis and machine learning workflows.

Why Data Cleaning Matters

Data cleaning improves the quality of analysis by reducing errors, inconsistencies, missing values, and misleading patterns.

Before Modelling

Before applying machine learning or statistical models, it is important to understand the structure, distribution, and limitations of the dataset.

Better Insights

Clean and well-prepared data supports clearer visualization, stronger interpretation, and more reliable conclusions.
