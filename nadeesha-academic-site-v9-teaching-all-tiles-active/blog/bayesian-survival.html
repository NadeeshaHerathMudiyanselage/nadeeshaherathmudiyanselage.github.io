What I Learned from Bayesian Survival Analysis

30 July 2026 · Statistics

My master's thesis investigated prior distribution approaches in Bayesian survival models. Survival analysis is especially useful when the outcome of interest is the time until an event occurs.

The role of prior distributions

In Bayesian modelling, prior distributions represent assumptions or knowledge before the observed data are incorporated. Their influence can be important, particularly in smaller or more complex datasets.

A practical lesson

Model assumptions should be examined rather than treated as invisible defaults. Sensitivity analysis and clear reporting help make statistical conclusions more transparent.
