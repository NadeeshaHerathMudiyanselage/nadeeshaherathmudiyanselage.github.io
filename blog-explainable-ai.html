Why Explainable AI Matters in Real-World Decision-Making

Exploring why transparency, interpretation, and visual analytics are important when machine learning models support practical decisions.

Beyond Accuracy

A model can achieve high accuracy, but decision-makers often need to understand why the model made a particular prediction.

Human-Centered AI

Explainable AI helps bridge the gap between computational models and human understanding by making patterns, uncertainty, and important features easier to interpret.

Visual Analytics

Visualization can support explainability by presenting complex model behaviour in a clearer and more accessible way.
