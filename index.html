About

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Helsinki, Finland. My PhD research focuses on developing explainable data mining algorithms under the supervision of Prof. Nikolaj Tatti and Dr. Florian Adriaens.

My broader research interests include explainable artificial intelligence, data mining, algorithms, machine learning, and statistical data analysis.
