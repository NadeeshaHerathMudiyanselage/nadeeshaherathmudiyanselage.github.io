About Me

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Helsinki, Finland. My PhD research focuses on developing explainable data mining algorithms under the supervision of Prof. Nikolaj Tatti and Dr. Florian Adriaens.

My broader interests include artificial intelligence and statistical data analysis techniques. I am particularly interested in applying these methods to real-world problems in areas such as public health, education, agriculture, and environmental science.
