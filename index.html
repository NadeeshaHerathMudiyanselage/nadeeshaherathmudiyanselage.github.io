About Me

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Helsinki, Finland where my PhD research focuses on developing explainable data mining algorithms under the supervision of Prof. Nikolaj Tatti and Dr. Florian Adriaens.

My broader interests include artificial intelligence and statistical data analysis techniques, with a focus on applying these methods to real-world problems in various areas including public health, education, agriculture, and environmental science.
