Undergraduate Level

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

ICT 307 · 2.0 Credits · Undergraduate

Overview

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the fundamental principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Students will explore the core concepts, historical evolution, and emerging trends shaping the field today. The course emphasizes foundational problem-solving methods, including search algorithms and knowledge representation techniques, enabling students to analyze and design intelligent systems. In addition to theoretical understanding, learners will gain hands-on experience by implementing AI concepts using Prolog, reinforcing their grasp of logic-based programming and AI reasoning. By the end of the course, students will have a solid grounding in essential AI methodologies and practical skills applicable to a wide range of computational problems.

Learning Objectives

  • To provide the fundamental knowledge to the students to understand what artificial intelligence is.
  • To introduce basic search algorithms and knowledge representation techniques used in artificial intelligence.
  • To test and strengthen the understanding of applications in AI using Prolog.

Topics Covered

  • Foundation of AI
  • Searching in AI
  • Knowledge representation
